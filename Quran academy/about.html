About Our Academy

Spreading Quran Education and Islamic Knowledge Worldwide

Welcome to Al Mohsin Quran Academy

Al Mohsin Quran Academy is an online Islamic educational platform dedicated to teaching the Holy Quran and Islamic knowledge to students around the world.

We provide online Quran classes for children and adults through Zoom. Our courses include Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading with Tajweed, Quran Memorization, Islamic Studies, Salah and Daily Duas, and Arabic Language.

Our aim is to make Quran education accessible and convenient for everyone, regardless of their location.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality Quran education and Islamic knowledge to Muslims worldwide. We aim to help students read the Holy Quran correctly, improve their Tajweed, memorize the Quran and understand the basic teachings of Islam.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted online Quran learning platform that connects students with quality Islamic education across different countries. We want to make learning the Holy Quran easier and more accessible for every family.
